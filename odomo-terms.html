2. Description of service

Odomo is a fuel and vehicle management app. It lets you log fuel and odometer entries, by photographing a receipt or dashboard, or by typing them in manually, and uses that history to show spending trends and predict upcoming refuel and service needs.

The app currently stores your data locally on your device. Some features described here, such as cloud sync, are planned but not yet available; the app will indicate clearly which features are live.

4. Accuracy of estimates

Predictive refuel and service reminders, and any figures read automatically from a photographed receipt or dashboard, are estimates only. They're calculated from the entries you've logged and may be wrong, incomplete, or out of date. Always confirm a reading before saving it, and don't rely on a reminder as your only signal that your vehicle needs fuel or service.
