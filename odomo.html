FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to type anything in manually?

No, if you use AI photo auto-fill: Odomo reads your fuel receipt or dashboard photo and pre-fills the entry, you just confirm it. Manual entry is always available and always free, if you'd rather type a reading in directly.

Is the AI photo scan free?

AI-powered auto-fill from a receipt or dashboard photo is a premium feature, available as an in-app purchase. Manual entry is free for everyone, with no purchase required.

Where is my data stored?

Your entries are stored locally on your device today. A cloud sync option is planned for a future release but is not live yet, so your data doesn't leave your phone right now.

What if a photo doesn't read correctly?

The confirm screen always shows what Odomo extracted before it's saved, so you can fix any field by hand before it's logged.

Which platforms does Odomo support?

Odomo is available now for iOS and Android, built from a single codebase. Download it using the App Store or Google Play badges above.
