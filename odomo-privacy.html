1. Overview

Odomo is a fuel and vehicle management app. It lets you photograph a fuel receipt or your vehicle's dashboard so you don't have to type odometer and fuel readings in by hand, and it uses your logged history to predict upcoming refuels and service. This policy explains what information the app works with, where it's stored, and how you're in control of it.

This is a standard-form template written from the app's actual data handling as of this build. It has not been reviewed by legal counsel. Replace the bracketed placeholders and have it reviewed before publishing it for real users.

2. Information we collect

Using Odomo involves the following information:

  • Vehicle and fuel entries you log or confirm: odometer readings, fuel volume and cost, dates, and the vehicles you add.
  • Photos you capture or select of fuel receipts and dashboards, used to read the numbers on them.
  • App preferences such as your chosen unit system and currency, set during onboarding.

We do not ask for your name, an account, or payment details to use the app today.

3. How we use your data

Your logged entries are used to:

  • Show your fuel and vehicle history back to you inside the app.
  • Calculate cost-per-distance and spending trends per vehicle.
  • Predict your next likely refuel and estimate upcoming service, based on your own logged history.
  • Pre-fill new entries from a receipt or dashboard photo, so you can confirm instead of typing.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell it.

4. Where your data lives

As of this build, all of your entries are stored locally on your device in an on-device database. There is no remote server or account system in active use yet, so your data does not leave your phone as part of normal app use.

A cloud sync option, to back up and sync your data across devices, is planned for a future release. If and when that ships, this policy will be updated first to describe what's transmitted, where it's hosted, and how to opt in or out, before the feature is turned on for you.

5. Camera & photo library access

Odomo requests camera access to let you photograph fuel receipts and your dashboard, and photo library access to let you pick an existing photo instead. These permissions are used only to capture or select the image you choose to scan. Odomo does not access your camera or photo library in the background, and captured photos are processed to read the numbers you're logging.

6. In-app purchases

AI-powered auto-fill from a photographed receipt or dashboard is a premium feature, available as an in-app purchase. Manual entry remains free for everyone, with no purchase required. Purchases are billed and processed by the App Store or Google Play; we don't receive or store your payment card details ourselves.

7. Sharing & third parties

Because entries are stored on-device today, we don't share your fuel or vehicle data with third parties. The AI auto-fill feature may send a captured photo to a text- or vision-based recognition service to read the numbers on it; when that's active, this section will name the specific provider(s) used and what is sent to them.

8. Retention & deletion

Your entries remain on your device for as long as the app is installed, or until you delete them individually within the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data along with it. Because there is no account or remote copy today, we have no separate copy of your data to delete on request.

9. Children's privacy

Odomo is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If how Odomo handles your data changes, most notably when cloud sync or a new extraction provider is introduced, we'll update this page and change the effective date above before the change takes effect.
